# Say It For Me — Because Sometimes You Know What You Mean, Just Not How to Say It

> **Project status:** Idea / early build concept  
> **Theme:** Build for a Friend  
> **Core idea:** Use open-source AI to help someone turn what they *mean* into something they can actually *say*.

---

## What I Built

**Say It For Me** is a personal communication assistant designed for a real problem: sometimes you know exactly what you feel or what you want to communicate, but you cannot find the right words.

This is especially common in everyday conversations:

- replying to an awkward message
- saying no without sounding rude
- apologizing without making excuses
- asking someone for help
- setting a boundary
- rescheduling plans
- supporting a friend
- replying when emotions make it difficult to think clearly

The idea is **not** to let AI speak for someone.

It is to help them express **their own intention in their own way**.

A user gives Say It For Me the message they received, explains what they want to communicate, and chooses how they want the response to sound.

For example:

> **Message:** "Are you coming tonight?"

> **Intent:** I can't come, but I don't want them to think I'm avoiding them.

> **Tone:** Friendly

Say It For Me could suggest a few natural responses rather than giving the user one "perfect" answer.

The user can then edit, combine, or completely ignore the suggestions.

### The person stays in control.

That is the central idea behind the project.

---

## Why This Problem?

Most AI writing tools are designed around productivity:

- write an email
- summarize something
- generate an essay
- write a professional message

But a lot of communication problems are much more personal than that.

The difficult part is often not **what to say**.

It is:

> **"How do I say this without sounding like someone I'm not?"**

A message can technically be correct and still feel completely wrong.

A friend might want to be direct but not cold.  
Casual but not careless.  
Funny but not insensitive.  
Honest but not unnecessarily harsh.

That is where Say It For Me is meant to help.
